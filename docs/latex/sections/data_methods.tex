\section{Data and Methods}\label{sec:data-methods}

\subsection{Experimental solubility and speciation data}
We compiled 161 equilibrium \COtwo partial-pressure records from six VLE sources\cite{Aronu2011,Hilliard2008,Idris2014,Jou1995,Mamun2005,Xu2011}.  Each row records the cited source and original row locator together with amine mass fraction, temperature, \COtwo loading, and measured pressure.  Six Xu et al.\ records use the authors' nominal temperatures: measurements at 100.5--\SI{101.1}{\degreeCelsius} are labeled \SI{100}{\degreeCelsius}, and measurements at 120.4--\SI{121.8}{\degreeCelsius} are labeled \SI{120}{\degreeCelsius}.

The speciation evidence contains 74 NMR states from three studies\cite{Bottinger2008,Jakobsen2005,Matin2012}.  We retained the measurement classification of each source value while converting reported composition bases to common feed and loading bookkeeping.  Across 666 species or aggregate entries, 219 positive species measurements and 74 positive \ce{MEA + MEAH+} aggregate measurements enter logarithmic residuals.  Fifteen source-reported zero \HCOthree values enter upper-bound checks; the sources provide no detection limits that would justify treating them as exact zeros.  The remaining 358 values were derived from material or charge balance and provide composition context only.

\subsection{Species basis and parameters}
The activity calculation uses the nine-species liquid basis in \cref{eq:true-species-vector}.  Molecular, water--ion, and SSM+DS parameters were taken from the literature cited in \cref{tab:full-ionic-ssm-ds-parameters,tab:binary-interaction-parameters}.  We fitted the \MEAH and \MEACOO segment diameters, dispersion energies, Born diameters, and their binary interaction parameter to NMR speciation data.

\input{tables/full_ionic_ssm_ds_parameters}

The NMR data directly measure \MEACOO at all 74 states and measure \MEA and \MEAH separately at 35 states each; the \ce{MEA + MEAH+} aggregate is available at all 74 states.  They also contain 53 positive and 15 source-reported zero \HCOthree observations and 16 positive \COthree observations.  \HthreeO and \Hydroxide occur only as balance-derived values.

\subsection{Pure-component and binary parameters}
The molecular \COtwo, \MEA, and \HtwoO parameters were taken from the cited PC-SAFT and ePC-SAFT studies.  The temperature-dependent water segment diameter is
\begin{equation}
    \sigma_{\HtwoO}(T)=2.7927+10.11\exp(-0.01775T)-1.417\exp(-0.01146T),
    \label{eq:water-sigma-temperature}
\end{equation}
where \(T\) is in kelvin and \(\sigma_{\HtwoO}\) is in angstroms.

The binary-interaction matrix is sparse.  \Cref{tab:binary-interaction-parameters} lists its nonzero terms; all unlisted pairs use \(k_{ij}=0\).

\subsection{Parameter estimation}
We fitted the segment diameter, dispersion energy, and Born diameter of \MEAH and \MEACOO, together with \(k_{\MEAH,\MEACOO}\).  The regression used 22 logarithmic composition residuals from eight NMR states: four from B\"ottinger et al., two from Jakobsen et al., and two from Matin et al.\ \cite{Bottinger2008,Jakobsen2005,Matin2012}.  These states span 20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} and \COtwo loadings from 0.075 to 0.929.  The objective gave equal weight to each residual and included a weak dimensionless regularization term of 0.003 relative to the initial parameter vector.

The nonlinear least-squares calculation used ePC-SAFT 1.5.2 for the activity and fugacity evaluations.  It satisfied the step-tolerance termination criterion after 13 objective evaluations.  The residual norm decreased from 0.27144 to 0.26766, and no fitted parameter reached its bound.  \Cref{tab:full-ionic-ssm-ds-parameters,tab:binary-interaction-parameters} report the resulting estimates.  The other 66 NMR states and all 161 pressure records were excluded from parameter estimation.

\subsection{Evaluation residuals}
The pressure residual is expressed on a logarithmic basis,
\begin{equation}
    \epsilon_{P,j}
    =
    \log_{10}\left(\frac{P_{\COtwo,j}^{\mathrm{model}}}{P_{\COtwo,j}^{\mathrm{exp}}}\right),
    \label{eq:pressure-log-residual}
\end{equation}
because measured \COtwo partial pressures span several orders of magnitude.  Positive measured species and aggregate values use the same \(\log_{10}(\mathrm{model}/\mathrm{data})\) form.  We compare source-reported zero values with modeled mole-fraction upper bounds and exclude balance-derived values from independent residuals.

\subsection{Computational implementation and reproducibility}
We solved the ideal baseline as eight reaction, material-balance, and charge residuals in an eight-variable softmax representation with SciPy nonlinear least squares.  The first loading uses a chemistry-informed positive composition; subsequent points at the same temperature start from the previous accepted composition.  Each solve permits at most 1000 residual evaluations and uses \(10^{-11}\) function, step, and gradient tolerances.  We accepted solutions whose largest unscaled residual did not exceed \(10^{-8}\).

The activity calculation uses the same ordered nine-species basis with \texttt{epcsaft} 1.5.2 pinned at Git commit \texttt{9f51afd0f9c11a6497ddca05c8b2dd0ea0ffa785}.  Reactive-speciation solves use at most 80 iterations, a \(10^{-7}\) solver tolerance, damping 0.5, and a minimum mole fraction of \(10^{-14}\).  Accepted solutions have mass-balance residuals at most \(10^{-7}\), charge residual at most \(10^{-6}\), reaction residuals at most \(10^{-7}\), finite positive normalized compositions, and no state-evaluation failure.  Reactive bubble calculations use at most 120 iterations, a \(10^{-6}\) fugacity tolerance, and pressure bounds from \(10^{-3}\) to \(10^{8}\) Pa.  Metrics and plots include only accepted solutions.
